 #### How is the quality of a MODCUBE home? Does it feel comfortable to live in?
 Although MODCUBE is delivered as a prefabricated product, all the building materials used are among the mainstream, high-quality materials found in Australian homes today. <br>In terms of overall construction quality and living experience, a MODCUBE home is fully on par with—or even superior to—a conventionally built house, especially in areas such as insulation, soundproofing, and wall strength.
