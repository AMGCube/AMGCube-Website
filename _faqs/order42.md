  #### Can I hire my own contractor for installation?
  Installation of MODCUBE homes requires specialized experience and equipment.<br> While we strongly recommend using our certified installation team, you may engage your own builder provided they obtain prior approval from MODCUBE’s project management. All external contractors must meet MODCUBE’s quality, safety, and scheduling standards.
