About Us

We come from all around the world, united by one wish:

That one day, everyone can have a home,
without waiting too long or,
sacrificing everything they have.

This day will arrive sooner because of AMGCube.

STORY

Our starting point and the path we've traveled.

  • OCTOBER 2023

    We Began

    In response to Australia's housing crisis, we took the first step toward change.

  • OCTOBER 2024

    Technical Prototype Completed

    We partnered with MODCUBE, a leading prefabricated product company in China, to develop a housing prototype tailored for the Australian market.

  • JANUARY 2025

    AMGCUBE launched operations

    Introduced the MODCUBE HOMES residential product tailored for the Australian market.

  • MAY 2025

    Display House Opening Soon

    Design completed, Approval granted. Construction expected to finish in July 2025.
