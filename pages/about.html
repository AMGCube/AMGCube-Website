01

Our thinking

Most homes are delivered as projects. We think they should be developed as products.

Many homes are still built as one-off projects. Designs, details, materials and construction processes are assembled again for each site, often with the same problems being solved repeatedly. What is learned on one project rarely survives into the next.

A product works differently. It is designed once, refined, and improved with each version. What has been resolved stays resolved.

02

Where value comes from

Design decides more than manufacturing does.

Offsite manufacturing and modular construction are ways of making a home. They are means, not the point.

We believe what makes a home good is settled long before anything is made — in how the plan works, how the home sits on its property, and how its details will hold up in ten years. A well-made home built from a poor design is still a poor home.

03

Why granny flats

A small home is still a complete home.

Granny flats are sometimes treated as secondary buildings, but the people living in them still need comfort, privacy, storage, natural light and spaces that work well every day.

Their smaller scale makes every decision more visible. There is less room for wasted space, unclear planning or details that have not been properly considered.

04

Where we are heading

Consistency belongs to the parts. Adaptation belongs to the property.

Learning from repetition does not mean treating every home as identical.

We are working toward that, one project at a time.

That direction is being developed through the projects we deliver today.
