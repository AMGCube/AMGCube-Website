What we believe

We believe a granny flat should be practical, comfortable and carefully planned.

We help Brisbane homeowners move from an idea to a completed home through a clear and coordinated building process.

STORY

  • OCTOBER 2023

    We Began

    AMGCUBE began by exploring practical ways to deliver well-designed small homes in Australia.

  • OCTOBER 2024

    Early Housing Prototype Completed

    Working with industry partners, we completed an early housing prototype to explore practical construction approaches for Australian conditions.

  • JANUARY 2025

    AMGCUBE Launched

    AMGCUBE HOMES was introduced with an increasing focus on practical granny-flat projects for Brisbane homeowners.

  • JUNE 2025

    Oxley Display Home Construction Began

    Construction began on our first Brisbane granny-flat display home in Oxley, putting our planning and delivery approach into practice on a local site.

  • JANUARY 2026

    Oxley Display Home Completed

    Our Oxley display home was completed and approved, giving Brisbane homeowners a finished AMGCUBE granny flat they can visit before planning their own project.

  • 20 July 2026

    QBCC Builder Licence Approved

    AMGCUBE PTY LTD was approved for a QBCC Builder – Low Rise licence, marking an important step in our ability to contract for and deliver local granny-flat projects in Queensland.
