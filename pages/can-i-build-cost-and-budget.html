What does the budget need to cover?

A useful budget needs to cover the complete project — not only the granny flat itself, but also the work required to design, approve, connect and build it on the property.

01

The home

  • Size, layout and design
  • Structure, inclusions and finishes
  • Kitchen, bathroom and internal services
  • Fixtures, fittings and internal works
02

The site

  • Access and site preparation
  • Earthworks and foundations
  • Drainage and service connections
  • Site-specific construction requirements
03

Approvals & external works

  • Design, engineering and consultants
  • Certification and required approvals
  • Council or authority-related requirements
  • Driveways, landscaping and other external works

A realistic overall starting point

From$250,000

For a typical 80m², two-bedroom, two-bathroom project in Brisbane, with straightforward site access and no unusual ground conditions.

Final pricing depends on the confirmed property and project scope.
Why we check the property before confirming a budget

The same floor plan can have a different total cost on two different properties. Once the site, inclusions and required external work are understood, we can prepare a clearer building proposal with the project scope properly set out.
