Cost & Budget

What might the project cost?

A useful budget needs to consider the complete project, not only the house itself.

01

The home

  • Size, layout and design
  • Inclusions and finishes
  • Fixtures and internal works
02

The site

  • Access and preparation
  • Earthworks and foundations
  • Services and connections
03

Approvals & external works

  • Design and consultants
  • Applications and certification
  • Landscaping and completion works

Why we check the property before confirming a budget

The same floor plan can have a different total project cost on two different properties. A property check helps identify the site-specific work that needs to be allowed for.
