Process & Timeline

What happens, and what will you need to do?

The project has two broad stages: preparing the property, design and approvals properly, then managing the construction and handover on site.

Phase 01

Property, design & approvals

  1. Step 01

    Property check

    We review the address, backyard, access and obvious property constraints to decide whether the project looks worth taking further.

  2. Step 02

    Initial direction & budget

    We discuss the brief, likely layout, level of finish and an initial view of the overall project budget.

  3. Step 03

    Design & investigations

    We coordinate the required survey, site investigations, design development and engineering for the property.

  4. Step 04

    Approvals & confirmed scope

    We prepare the required documentation, coordinate the approval pathway and confirm the scope before construction begins.

Phase 02

Construction & handover

  1. Step 05

    Construction

    We manage the site, trades, deliveries, inspections and progress updates throughout the build.

  2. Step 06

    Inspection & handover

    We complete the final inspections, address outstanding items and hand over the completed granny flat.

The programme is confirmed once the required investigations, approval pathway and project scope are known. Timing can vary with the property, approvals, site conditions, weather and client decisions.
