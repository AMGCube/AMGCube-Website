Process & Timeline

How does the project move forward?

The programme has two distinct parts: preparing the project properly, then completing the work on site.

Phase 01

Pre-construction

  1. Step 01

    Property check

    Review the property, constraints and opportunities.

  2. Step 02

    Plan & initial budget

    Develop the brief, concept direction and budget range.

  3. Step 03

    Design & investigations

    Coordinate the design and the site-specific information required.

  4. Step 04

    Approvals & documentation

    Prepare documentation and coordinate the applicable approval pathway.

Phase 02

On-site construction

  1. Step 05

    Construction

    Build the granny flat with coordinated site management and communication.

  2. Step 06

    Inspection & handover

    Complete final inspections, practical completion and handover.

Timing is confirmed for each property and depends on the required investigations, approvals, consultants, site conditions, weather and client decisions.
