What happens, and what will you need to do?

The project has two broad stages: preparing the property, design and approvals properly, then managing the construction and handover on site.

Phase 01

Property, design & approvals

  1. Step 01

    Property check

    We review the address, backyard, access and obvious property constraints to decide whether the project looks worth taking further.

  2. Step 02

    Survey, design & structural

    We carry out a site survey and soil test, then develop the structural design and confirm the home meets energy efficiency requirements under the National Construction Code.

  3. Step 03

    Design finalisation

    We finalise the complete set of drawings and technical specifications, including internal fit-out choices such as kitchen, bathroom and window details.

  4. Step 04

    Quotation & sign-off

    We prepare a full quotation based on the finalised design, setting out the total cost and payment stages. Once you approve the design and price, we move to a construction contract.

Phase 02

Construction & handover

  1. Step 05

    Contract & approval

    We sign a formal construction contract confirming price, timing and payment terms, then arrange the building approval required before work can start on site.

  2. Step 06

    Construction

    Construction proceeds through each stage — from foundations through to internal fit-out — with inspections at every stage before moving to the next.

  3. Step 07

    Inspection & handover

    We complete final inspections, resolve any outstanding items, and hand the finished home over to you.

The programme is confirmed once the required investigations, approval pathway and project scope are known. Timing can vary with the property, approvals, site conditions, weather and client decisions.
