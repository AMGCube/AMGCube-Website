What happens, and what will you need to do?

The project has two broad stages: preparing the property, design and approvals properly, then managing the construction and handover on site.

• Project starts here
Preliminary— demand, design & quote
1

Property check

Address, backyard and access reviewed.

2

Survey, design & structural

Site survey, soil test, structural design, energy compliance.

3

Design finalisation

Drawings and fit-out specifications confirmed.

4

Quotation & sign-off

Quotation prepared, design and price approved.

Construction & handover— building and delivering the home
5

Contract & approval

Construction contract signed, building approval arranged.

6

Construction

Foundations through internal fit-out, inspected at every stage.

7

Inspection & handover

Final inspections complete, home handed over.

✓ Home handed over

The programme is confirmed once the required investigations, approval pathway and project scope are known. Timing can vary with the property, approvals, site conditions, weather and client decisions.
