Size & Layout

How big can your granny flat be?

The maximum size you can build is set by your local council — and it varies from one council to the next. Two similar-looking blocks in different areas can have quite different limits.

We check the rules that apply to your specific block, then shape the layout around who will live there and how the space needs to work day to day.

A council-by-council size guide is on its way

In the meantime, send us your address and we'll tell you what applies to your block.

What shapes the layout

Every layout is custom. What makes that possible is how we build it.

The right layout depends on three things working together — your needs, what the site allows, and what the budget covers. No two granny flats end up quite the same.

What makes that customisation possible without driving up cost or time is how we build. Each home is assembled from a set of standardised components, refined and proven across projects. The design is tailored to you; the parts that build it are not reinvented each time.

Most granny flats have one to three bedrooms and one to three bathrooms, and are built as a single storey. A two-storey layout is possible where the site and design call for it.
