The three questions to consider first

A practical starting point for your property.

Before choosing a floor plan, it helps to understand whether the property can work, what the complete project may cost, and what needs to happen before construction begins.

A Site Feasibility

Can this property realistically accommodate a granny flat?

A large backyard does not always mean there is a practical place to build. The usable space, construction access and site conditions all need to be considered together.

  • Is there enough usable space?
  • Can machinery, materials and trades reach the backyard?
  • Could the site conditions significantly increase the cost?

How much should I realistically budget?
