Feasibility · Budget · Process

We answer the questions that decide everything — before you pick a floor plan.

It's tempting to start with a floor plan. But whether a granny flat works, what it should cost and how long it takes are all settled earlier — by the property itself, and by the order the decisions are made in.

So we work through four practical questions first: can it be built, how big it can be, what it will cost, and how long the whole process takes. Getting these right early is what keeps a project from stalling — or costing more — later on.

Most of this is our job, not yours. You don't need to work out council rules, site conditions or approvals on your own. We do that with you, and tell you plainly where things stand.

A Can it be built?

Will it work in your backyard?

We assess your block together — access, slope, services and setbacks — and tell you honestly whether it's worth taking further. You send the address and a few photos; we do the reading.

B How big, and what layout?

How much space do you need?

The size you're allowed is set by your council, and differs from one area to the next. We check what applies to your block, then shape the layout around who will live there.

C What will it cost?

What will it actually cost?

We explain what shapes the final figure — site works, services, approvals, finishes — so the number makes sense. Once the scope is clear, you get a fixed-price proposal, not a moving estimate.

D How long does it take?

How long will the complete project take?

We walk you through the full programme — every stage from first chat to handover — so the timeline is a path you can follow, not just a number. Most of the waiting happens before the build even starts.
