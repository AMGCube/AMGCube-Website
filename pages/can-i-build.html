Feasibility · Budget · Process

We answer the questions that decide everything first.

It's tempting to start with a floor plan. But whether a granny flat works, what it costs and how long it takes are decided earlier — by the property, and by the order the decisions are made in.

Most of that is our job, not yours. You don't need to work out council rules, site conditions or approvals on your own — we do that with you, and tell you plainly where things stand.

A Can it be built?

We assess your block together — access, slope, services and setbacks — and tell you honestly whether it's worth taking further.

B How big, and what layout?

The size you can build is set by your council, and differs from one area to the next. We check what applies to your block.

C What will it cost?

Site works, services, approvals and finishes all shape the final figure. Once the scope is clear, you get a fixed-price proposal — not a moving estimate.

We walk you through every stage, from first chat to handover. Most of the waiting happens before the build even starts.
