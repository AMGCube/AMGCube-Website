The three questions we answer first

Most of the technical work is our job, not yours.

Before choosing a floor plan, we first look at three things: whether the property is suitable, what the complete project is likely to cost, and what needs to happen before construction begins.

We work through these with you at the start, so you can make decisions with a clearer picture of the site, budget and process.

A Site Feasibility

Will it work in your backyard?

A large backyard does not automatically mean a granny flat will work. Access, slope, setbacks and existing services can matter just as much as the available space.

  • Usable building space after setbacks and services are considered
  • Side access width, slope and existing structures
  • Site conditions that could meaningfully affect the design or cost

Send us the address and a few photos of the backyard and side access. We’ll tell you whether the property looks worth taking further.

B Cost & Budget

What will it actually cost?

The floor plan is only one part of the final price. Site works, services, approvals and finishes can all affect the overall budget, which is why similar-looking granny flats may still cost different amounts.

A realistic starting point From $250,000 For a typical completed project, including design, engineering, approvals, construction and handover. Final pricing depends on the site and agreed inclusions.

Once the property and scope are confirmed, we can prepare a fixed-price building proposal with the inclusions and any site-specific work clearly set out.

  • What is included in the starting price
  • Which site conditions can change the final cost

C Process & Timeline

What will you need to do?

Your main decisions are the layout, finishes and any changes you would like to make.

We manage the technical work, approvals, construction and handover, and let you know whenever something needs your input.

  • We assess the property and confirm what is practical
  • We manage design, engineering, certification and approvals
  • We coordinate trades, inspections, construction and handover

You’ll receive clear progress updates throughout the project, without having to manage the construction process yourself.
