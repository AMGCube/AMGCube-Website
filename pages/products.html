MODCUBE HOMES

MODCUBE HOMES is a next-generation prefabricated residential building solution, integrating a heavy steel frame, advanced enclosure systems, and modular interior fit-outs to enable high-quality, efficient construction with fast turnaround.

Each MODCUBE unit is a precisely engineered structural module, factory-built and pre-integrated with core components including the frame, external walls, kitchen, bathroom, electrical and plumbing systems, and interior finishes—delivered fully assembled and move-in ready.

Using off-site prefabrication methods, MODCUBE requires only basic foundations and on-site module installation, dramatically reducing build time and labour costs while improving quality assurance and environmental performance.

MODCUBE can be used as a detached house, backyard granny flat, or scaled into townhouses, eco resorts, workforce camps, student accommodation, and more.
