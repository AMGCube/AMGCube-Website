AMGCUBE is a Brisbane-based QBCC-licensed builder focused on granny flats. We start with your property and help you move from an initial assessment to design, approvals, construction and completion.

Every property and project is different. Our role is to establish a practical direction based on your site, priorities, budget and the requirements that apply to the project.

01

Property Feasibility

Before design work begins, we help you identify whether your property may be suitable for a granny flat and what constraints could influence the project.

This initial review may consider planning requirements, overlays, setbacks, site access, slope, existing buildings, available services and other visible or publicly available constraints. It is an early project review rather than formal planning, surveying or engineering advice, and does not constitute an approval or guarantee of feasibility.

  • Initial property and project review
  • Identification of important site and planning considerations
  • Guidance on the likely next steps and approval pathway
02

Practical Scope & Planning

A well-planned project starts with a clear understanding of what is proposed. We work with you to define the layout, inclusions, finishes and site work that form the project scope.

Cost and timing depend on the design, approvals, access, site conditions, selected finishes and other project requirements. Establishing the scope early helps set realistic expectations and reduce avoidable changes later.

  • Practical layouts shaped around the property and intended use
  • Clear project scope, inclusions and site work
  • Realistic expectations for cost and timing
03

Construction & Delivery

As the licensed builder, AMGCUBE manages construction within Queensland's building and approval framework. We work against the approved design and agreed scope, coordinating the relevant trades, materials, inspections and construction stages.

We maintain clear communication as work progresses and address practical site issues as they arise. Decisions that affect the approved design or agreed scope are discussed as part of the construction process.

  • Construction managed against approved documentation
  • Coordination of relevant trades, materials and stages
  • Clear communication throughout the build
04

Compliance & Completion

Construction must follow the approved documentation and the requirements that apply to the project. Relevant licensed trades are engaged and required inspections are completed at the appropriate stages.

Consultants, certifiers and inspectors perform their respective roles independently as required. AMGCUBE manages the building process through final inspections, completion and handover, while keeping the homeowner informed of the steps involved.

  • Construction in accordance with approved documentation
  • Relevant licensed trades and required inspections
  • Coordination through to completion and handover
